About:
FlySpy is an open-source, AI-powered First-Person View (FPV) drone platform developed as part of the Master’s Project – Intelligent Systems (SS2025) at Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences.
The goal of the project is to design a cost-effective and modular drone system capable of performing real-time human detection, with practical applications in reconnaissance, academic research, and educational use.

Unlike commercial systems that rely on proprietary and costly components, FlySpy is built using accessible hardware, open-source software, and features a browser-based control dashboard. This approach ensures that the system remains both powerful and easily reproducible, making it ideal for experimentation and further development.


Drone Structure and components:
  • Architecture divided into three layers:
	o Onboard drone hardware
	o Video streaming setup
	o Ground station AI system
  • Drone Frame:
	o Built on a 5-inch FlyFishRC Volador II VX5 O3 frame
  • Core Electronics:
	o Flight Controller: SpeedyBee F405 AIO 40A
	o FPV Camera: Caddx Ratel Pro (analog)
	o Video Transmitter (VTX): SpeedyBee TX800
	o Receiver: ELRS (e.g., Radiomaster RP1)
	o Remote Controller: Radiomaster BOXER

  • Power System:
	o Motors: Xing E-Pro motors
	o Propellers: HQProp T3.5x2x3 tri-blade (3.5 inch)
	o Battery: 3–6S LiPo

  • Video Streaming Setup:
	o FPV feed sent to Skyzone Cobra X V4 goggles
	o Video output routed via RCA → USB capture card → laptop
	o Decouples flight control from video processing
	o Ensures low-latency, stable streaming

  • Ground Station (Laptop):
	o Runs AI human detection pipeline (e.g., MobileNetSSD)
	o Connects to drone via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
	o Displays telemetry in real time:
	o Viewable via interactive browser dashboard
