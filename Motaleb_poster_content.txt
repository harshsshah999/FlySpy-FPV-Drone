
🚧 Challenges in FPV Drone Projects
🔋 Short Battery Life: Most FPV drones fly only 3–7 minutes per charge.
📶 Signal Interference: Video/control, GPS signals can be affected by buildings or obstacles.
🎮 High Learning Curve: Difficult to control; requires practice and simulators.
💥 Crashes and Maintenance: Drones crash often; need frequent repairs and spare parts.
📜 Legal Restrictions: Strict laws in many countries about drone use, especially FPV.
🌧️ Weather Limitations: Wind, rain, and cold can affect performance or damage electronics.
⏱️ Latency in Video Feed: Delay in the video signal impacts real-time control.
💸 Cost of Equipment: FPV gear (goggles, controllers, batteries) is expensive.

💸 Budget-Related Challenges
🔧 High Initial Investment: FPV setup (drone, transmitter, goggles, batteries) can cost €300–€600 even for beginners.
🔋 Frequent Battery Replacements: LiPo batteries degrade quickly and need replacing after 50–100 cycles.
🧰 Repair & Spare Part Costs: Crashes are common—motors, arms, propellers, and frames often need replacement.
🧠 Simulator & Training Tools: Extra cost for flight simulators and compatible controllers to learn safely.
📦 Shipping & Import Fees: Many quality parts are only available online with high shipping/customs fees.
🧑‍🔧 Special Tools & Equipment: Requires soldering tools, battery chargers, FPV monitor, and calibration gear.

🔭 Future Scope and Innovations
🤖 AI-Based Autonomous Flight: Object tracking, follow-me mode, and obstacle avoidance.
🔋 Improved Battery Technology: Graphene or solid-state batteries enable longer flight time.
🛡️ Enhanced Safety Features: Auto-landing, collision prevention, and return-to-home systems.
🛸 Swarm Drones: Multiple drones flying in sync for shows, surveillance, and rescue.
🎓 FPV for Education & Training: Useful in robotics, coding, and drone engineering studies.
🎥 Commercial Applications: Cinematography, bridge/tower inspections, and precision farming.
📡 Integration with 5G: Ultra-low latency for high-speed video transmission and control.
📦 Miniaturized Smart Drones: Small, indoor-friendly drones with smart features.
☁️ Cloud-Based Flight Logging: Stores flight data for performance tracking and analysis.
